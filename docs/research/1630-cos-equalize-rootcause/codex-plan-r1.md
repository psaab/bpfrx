2. **MAJOR: “quantum rounding explains solo 1G = 84%” is not mechanically proven and looks numerically weak.**  
   `cos_guarantee_quantum_bytes` is `rate * 200us`, clamped to `[1500, 512K]` ([queue_service/mod.rs](/home/ps/git/bpfrx/.claude/worktrees/1630-research-cos-equalize-rootcause/userspace-dp/src/afxdp/cos/queue_service/mod.rs:1534)). The drain does stop when `remaining_secondary < len` ([drain.rs](/home/ps/git/bpfrx/.claude/worktrees/1630-research-cos-equalize-rootcause/userspace-dp/src/afxdp/cos/queue_service/drain.rs:216)), but the plan needs telemetry or a calculation that matches 84%. Right now, a simpler explanation is some other service-loop, TX-ring, flow-fair, V_min, or cadence limiter.

   The documented contract says Phase 2 distributes residual across queues not fully honored in Phase 1 ([fairness-regimes.md](/home/ps/git/bpfrx/.claude/worktrees/1630-research-cos-equalize-rootcause/docs/fairness-regimes.md:848)).
