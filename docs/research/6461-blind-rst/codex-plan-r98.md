2. **BLOCKER — a shared-row-only marker cannot survive the worker-replica purge topology or reach the cache gate.** Import fans the entry to every worker (`userspace-dp/src/afxdp/ha/session_import.rs:215-223`; `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/session_glue/commands/upsert_synced.rs:64-79`).
