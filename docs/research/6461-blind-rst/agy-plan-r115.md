## Verbatim review

UNSOUND

1. `docs/research/6461-blind-rst/plan.md:1267-1271` & `docs/research/6461-blind-rst/plan.md:1310-1323`: **Unmarked Forward Anchor on SYN-ACK-Driven Site-2b Synthesis Accepts a Closing Segment.**
   The round-115 fold introduces a strong OPENING proof requirement for SYN-ACK-driven reverse synthesis (`install_reverse_session_from_forward_match` at site 2b). On proof success, site 2b installs the reverse companion and marks the local forward entry in hand (lines 1303-1309). However, if the driving SYN-ACK packet itself also carries a closing flag (e.g. `SYN-ACK-RST` or `SYN-ACK-FIN`), §5.2 rule 5 explicitly states that "closing packets never promote — at all" (line 948) and "a closing-flagged packet never reaches `update_session`" (line 1236). But at site 2b, when a closing-flagged SYN-ACK arrives:
   - The packet validates against the forward entry's anchor (lines 1295-1298).
   - If accepted, line 1303 specifies: "install with `closing`/`reset` seeded as today, AND the mark is applied to the FORWARD family in the same resolve".
   - But if the forward entry was in `OPENING` state (`established = false`), marking the forward entry as `closing`/`reset` converts the forward entry to a closing session without ever running an establishment or ownership promote.
   - More critically, if a subsequent reverse closing segment arrives while the reverse entry is retained via companion retention (`expire.rs:318`, lines 1320-1323), line 1309 states that companion retention defers the reverse's 2 s reap while the forward companion is live (≤ 20 s opening window). Because the forward companion was marked on site-2b accept, the reverse companion retention allows an unproven or proof-passing SYN-ACK+RST to pin the half-open forward entry in closing state across the full 20 s window, creating a zero-producer / retained-zombie mismatch where the forward entry remains active and keeps the reverse entry alive via `companion_keeps_alive`.

2. `docs/research/6461-blind-rst/plan.md:1910-1927` & `docs/research/6461-blind-rst/plan.md:1943-1949`: **Family Binding Token Verification Gap in Reverse->Forward `account_packet` Hop.**
   In §5.8 (lines 1943-1949), the token producer contract specifies that the reverse->forward `account_packet`-style hop (`session/mod.rs:1177-1205`) re-verifies the forward entry's identity (`SessionKey`, `NatDecision`, `install_epoch`) before writing the anchor sample to prevent samples from landing on a replacement forward entry `K2`. However, `account_packet` in `userspace-dp/src/session/mod.rs:1183-1204` retrieves `fwd_key = reverse_session_key(&record_key, nat)` using only `record_key` and `nat` of the reverse entry. The reverse entry does NOT store the forward entry's `install_epoch`. Therefore, `account_packet` can verify that `entry.key == fwd_key` and `entry.decision.nat == nat`, but it CANNOT compare the forward entry's `install_epoch` against any expected epoch token, because no `install_epoch` of the forward companion was bound or stored inside the reverse entry at install time (`install.rs:152`). As a result, if a forward entry `K1` reaps and a new forward generation `K2` is installed under the exact same 5-tuple key and NAT decision, `account_packet` will match `K2`'s key and NAT decision and mistakenly update `K2`'s anchor sample, violating the epoch-binding invariant established for token verification.

### Summary of Review
The review of the plan document `docs/research/6461-blind-rst/plan.md` at version v10.31.0 reveals that while the token discipline, atomic compare-then-mutate helper, and site-2b OPENING proof structure are thoroughly specified across most surfaces, critical edge-case interactions between companion retention (`companion_keeps_alive`), epoch tracking on reverse entries, and closing-flagged SYN-ACKs introduce consistency issues.
