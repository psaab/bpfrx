Moreover, `drain_pending_tx` has no `SessionTable`, while the exact ring-committed prefix is known only in finalization. The target can subsequently discard through CoS admission, bounded local queues, cleanup, or TX submission. Applying on source-side inbox success therefore learns an undelivered packet; applying at target ring commit requires the cross-worker callback the plan explicitly rejects. Applying the original segment summary on the first child advances the anchor over unsent bytes; attaching it once can miss the transmitted child; cloning it applies the wrong range repeatedly. The plan needs per-child summaries or an explicit committed-child aggregation rule. (`userspace-dp/src/afxdp/tx/dispatch/mod.rs:590-700`; `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/tx/tcp_segmentation.rs:147-163,208-213,311-334`; `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/tx/transmit/finalise.rs:47-55`)
