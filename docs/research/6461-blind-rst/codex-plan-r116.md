1. **BLOCKER** — The site-2b rule gates on packet flags instead of the matched forward entry’s state. With an OPENING forward entry and no reverse entry, a spoofed ACK/PSH-ACK misses normal lookup, reaches reverse synthesis, bypasses the SYN-ACK-only proof, and installs an ESTABLISHED reverse entry with the full timeout. Its activity can then retain the OPENING forward half—the exact bare-reverse-ACK pin that normal lookup explicitly prevents. Conversely, an already-ESTABLISHED pickup/import may lack an OPENING proof interval, so the blanket SYN-ACK check can reject legitimate repair. Require proof for any non-closing reverse synth against an OPENING forward entry; preserve midstream pickup only when the forward is already established.
