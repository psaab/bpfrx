# #9024 — the duplicate-block census reported SILENT: 0 over 9 of 139 containers

- **Timestamp**: 2026-09-05
  - **Action**: the collector dropped every eligible container it could not
    build a two-block fixture for, BEFORE the census ran — so those containers
    reached neither the population nor the named skip list, and the skip ratchet
    could not guard them. **The ratchet guards the population; the population
    was filtered first.**
  - **Measured**: 139 eligible containers, 85 dropped by the pre-filter (61%).
    The census examined 9 and reported `SILENT: 0`.
  - **Fix**: an eligible container with no constructible fixture is now a
    RECORDED skip carrying its reason, distinguishing "container-only children
    (no scalar leaf to duplicate)" from "no two synthesizable single-value leaf
    children" — different facts with different futures. All 85 are pinned in
    `dupConservationSkipped8436`, so the existing ratchet fails on a new one and
    on a stale one.
  - **Both confirmed defects from #9023 are now visible** as named unprobed
    entries: `forwarding-options sampling instance` and `snmp trap-group`.
    Honest state — the census still cannot CHECK them, and now says so by name
    instead of by omission.
  - **The census prints its denominator**: `SILENT: 0` over a population of 9
    reads identically to `SILENT: 0` over 139, and the difference was the whole
    finding.
  - **Corrected a true-but-load-bearing hedge** in the inventory header. "Every
    container the census could reach" was accurate and carried 130 containers;
    a reader arrives at "this class is handled". It now states the reach.
  - **One claim in the issue was stale**: the skip buckets are NOT
    count-only — `dupConservationSkipped8436` and its ratchet already existed
    (added after `services rpm probe xpfname test` hid in a count for seven
    batches). The blind spot was upstream of that machinery, not a gap in it.
  - **File(s)**: pkg/config/duplicate_block_conservation_8436_test.go,
    pkg/config/duplicate_block_conservation_inventory_8436.go
