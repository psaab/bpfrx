## Why these were invisible

`#8879`'s population comes from `TestSweepFull`, which enumerates sites by
RUNNING the elision pass. A `(container, head)` pair the pass never asks about
produces no site, appears in no census, **and its absence there is
indistinguishable from having been adjudicated and cleared.**

Walking `setSchema` directly instead — the declared grammar rather than the
pass's behaviour — gives 474 admitted / 494 un-admitted pairs, of which only
**12 are top-level**. Drop-testing those twelve by hand found four live silent
drops:

| pair | braced | elided | warns b/e | strict b/e |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `class-of-service forwarding-classes` | `fc=1` | `fc=0` | 0/0 | ok/ok |
| `policy-options as-path` | `asp=1` | `asp=0` | 0/0 | ok/ok |
| `services application-identification` | `appid=true` | **`appid=false`** | 1/0 | ok/ok |
| `system internet-options` | `iopt=true` | `<nil>` | 0/0 | ok/ok |

`services application-identification` silently turns **DPI-based application
detection off** — the same shape as `security policy-stats` from batch 5.

## The census is landed as a guard, not as prose

`TestUnadmittedTopLevelPairsAreAdjudicated8925` walks `setSchema` and requires
every un-admitted top-level pair to be registered with a reason. The `no-body`
reason is **re-derived from the schema every run**, so a pair that GAINS
children — the transition that makes it droppable and re-opens this class — reds
the cell rather than passing quietly.

It caught an error immediately: I registered `policy-options as-path-group` from
memory and the "must not outlive its reason" arm rejected it as not being in the
set at all.

## The better remedy was tried and rejected ON EVIDENCE

`policy-options as-path` is `args: 2, multi: true`, so admitting it puts the
pair in the `#8880` unsplittable population — a packed run carrying two
`as-path` statements truncates to the first. Measured:

    braced                            before 2   after 2
    elided, two separate statements   before 0   after 2   <- fixed
    elided, both in ONE packed run    before 0   after 1   <- partial

Adding `packedStatements: true` to `schemaPolicyOptions` **does** fix the
one-run case completely (1 → 2) and shrank the population 460 → 457. I was
convinced. Then `TestPackedOptInHoldsForEveryLeafPair8768` failed for **every
leaf pair involving `community` or `policy-statement`** — both declare children,
so the split cannot find their boundary. The opt-in would have fixed `as-path`
by breaking two other leaves.

**Withdrawn.** The opt-in was a claim I had already convinced myself of on the
strength of one leaf, and #8768 exists precisely to make that claim testable.

The ratchet is therefore bumped 95→96 / 460→461 rather than tightened, with the
growth attributed: the pair joins the population because it is now REACHABLE at
all, and the loss it leaves (1 of 2) is smaller than the one it replaced (0 of
2).

## A claim in the ratchet's own message is narrowed

That cell's failure text generalises from `system name-server` that a `multi`
leaf makes `packedStatements` insufficient. `as-path` is `multi` and
`packedStatements` fixes it completely — being `multi` is not by itself what
makes the opt-in insufficient. Recorded next to the constant rather than
rewritten: the message's 89/373 split is lane-8388's measurement, not mine to
re-derive.
