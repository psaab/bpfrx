# #9030 — #3112's DNAT ICMP reversal was unreachable: the gate tested SNAT only

- **Timestamp**: 2026-09-06
  - **Action**: widened the embedded-ICMP reversal gate in
    `poll_descriptor/embedded_icmp.rs` from `rewrite_src.is_none()` to "no
    translation of any kind", so a pure-DNAT flow reaches the reversal.

    Closed #3112 landed the destination-side builders, their struct fields and
    their regression cells. The production caller could not reach any of it: a
    pure-DNAT decision is `rewrite_dst: Some(_), rewrite_src: None`, so the
    SNAT-only gate returned `NotHandled` for exactly the scenario #3112 was
    filed about. The existing #3112 cells call the builders DIRECTLY, which is
    why they stayed green over a dead path.

    **This is a pure reachability change and touches no builder** — measured,
    not assumed. The builder is already complete for the widened set: the
    destination ADDRESS restore is guarded by `had_dst_nat` (`rewrite_dst`), the
    destination PORT restore by `rewrite_dst_port || rewrite_dst`
    (`builders.rs:150`), and the source restores by `rewrite_src_port ||
    rewrite_src`. Each arm tests its own field, so admitting DNAT-only,
    port-only or static-NAT flows cannot half-restore a quote — an arm whose
    field is `None` simply does not fire. I checked this before widening
    precisely because a gate that admits a case the builder cannot serve would
    turn a missing reversal into a corrupted one.

    A correction to my own reading on the way: a narrow `sed` window over
    `builders.rs` showed no destination-port arm and I nearly widened only to
    the address case. `original_dst_port` is in fact read three times in that
    file, including the restore at :150. The windowed read was the error, not
    the code.

    **HA wire: nothing moves.** This changes no `SessionKey` field and no wire
    struct. The reversed error carries `flow_key: None` and seeds no session
    (asserted in the cell), so it is not a session-sync participant at all.

    Regression drives the REAL call site (`poll_binding_process_descriptor`),
    not the builders, since bypassing the caller is what let the defect survive.
    Two mutants, each on its own arm: reverting the gate to
    `rewrite_src.is_none()` reds the "must reach the reversal" assertion, and
    forcing `had_dst_nat = false` reds the "inner destination restored to the
    VIP" assertion — so the cell binds the gate AND the destination-restore arm
    rather than only the former.
