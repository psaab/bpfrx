## The design constraint that decided the fix, found by reading the callers

My first attempt keyed the flow on a **hash of the routing-instance name**,
because that name is what `NatScopeCtx` carries and the key is `Copy`. **That
would have been wrong**, and the reason is not stylistic:

Three of the four sites that build `SourceNatFlowKey` — the release path, the
HA-synced reserve, and the NAT64 arms — build it **from a `SessionKey`**, and
`synced.rs` states outright that its flow must be *"byte-identical to the
active's SNAT-match tuple"*. A scope derived a different way at the match site
would disagree with what those three compute, and **the standby would fail to
reserve the flow the active reserved** — turning a cross-tenant collision into a
sync defect.

So the scope is `SessionKey.routing_domain`, threaded through `NatScopeCtx`
rather than re-derived. Re-deriving it at the match site is not possible anyway:
`ingress_routing_domain` needs the ingress VLAN id and the fabric-encoded zone,
neither of which that site has.

## NAT64 is pinned at 0, deliberately and in both directions

The NAT64 allocation path carries no routing domain in its signature. Its
allocation and release sites now share one named constant, because **if they
disagreed the release would silently stop matching the allocation and leak the
pool entry** — worse than the collision being fixed. Zero preserves today's
behaviour exactly; NAT64 pools are per-prefix and are not the shared-pool
multi-VRF shape this issue describes.

## What was deliberately NOT changed

`AddressOnlyReverseKey` stays domain-agnostic. #9075's calibration of the source
report records that adding `routing_domain` there would *create* the cross-tenant
hijack it was meant to prevent.

## The modularity gate, answered by splitting rather than by a note

Adding the field pushed `source/mod.rs` from 1499 to 1534 LOC, past the 1500
[WATCH] floor. `docs/refactoring-audit-accepted.txt` exists for that, and a note
would have been the cheap answer — but the escape hatch is satisfiable by
writing something false, and there was a real seam here.

The two keys moved to `source/keys.rs`: everything there is **identity**,
everything left behind is **behaviour**, and the two keys this issue is about now
sit together because they had the same bug at two scopes. mod.rs is 1479.

minting a fresh one per packet, which drains the pool.
